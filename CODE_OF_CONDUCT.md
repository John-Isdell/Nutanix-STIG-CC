# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, welcoming, and harassment-free project
community. Participation is open to people of every background, identity,
experience level, and ability who engage in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss technical decisions and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private, customer, security, and infrastructure information.
- Respect maintainers' need to prioritize safety and reviewability.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or unwelcome sexual
  attention.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, customer data, sensitive infrastructure details, or
  exploit information through an inappropriate public channel.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, and public spaces where someone officially represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a private
contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue
that exposes private information or creates additional harm.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, limit
participation, or temporarily or permanently ban participants. Responses will
be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and focused on the
safety of affected people and the project community.
